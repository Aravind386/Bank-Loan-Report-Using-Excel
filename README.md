
# 🏦 Bank Loan Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Excel

### 📊 Loan Applications | Funded Amount | Loan Repayments | Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Bank Loan Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyse loan applications, funded amounts, amounts received, interest rates, debt-to-income (DTI) ratios, and loan repayment performance.

The dashboard provides an overview of loan portfolio performance, compares good and bad loans, and examines loan applications by purpose, employment length, term, state, loan grade, and loan status.

The project aims to turn loan data into meaningful insights that support portfolio monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse total loan applications and monthly application trends.
- Monitor total funded amounts and amounts received.
- Evaluate average interest rates and debt-to-income ratios.
- Compare good loans and bad loans.
- Analyse loan applications by loan purpose, grade, term, and employment length.
- Examine loan application distribution across states.
- Compare loan performance by loan status.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for loan portfolio reporting.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Overall Loan Performance

- The dashboard reports approximately **38.6K loan applications**.
- The total funded amount is approximately **₹435.8M**.
- The total amount received is approximately **₹473.1M**.
- The average interest rate is **12.05%**.
- The average debt-to-income (DTI) ratio is **13.33%**.

These KPIs provide an overview of lending activity, funds issued, amounts received, and borrower debt burden.

### 2. Good Loans vs Bad Loans

The summary dashboard classifies loans into good and bad loan groups.

| Metric | Good Loans | Bad Loans |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loan Applications | 33.2K | 5.3K |
| Funded Amount | ₹370.2M | ₹65.5M |
| Amount Received | ₹435.8M | ₹37.3M |
| Application Share | 86.18% | 13.82% |

- Good loans account for approximately **86.18%** of applications.
- Bad loans account for approximately **13.82%** of applications.
- The dashboard reports a larger funded amount and amount received for the good loan group.
- Bad loans account for a smaller share of applications but remain important for credit risk monitoring.

*The good/bad loan classification depends on the definitions used in the source data. Validate the categories and calculations before drawing conclusions about credit quality.*

### 3. Loan Application Trends

- The overview dashboard displays monthly loan application trends from January through December.
- The reported monthly application count increases from **2,332 in January** to **4,314 in December**.
- December is the highest month shown in the monthly trend chart.
- The month-over-month KPIs provide additional information about recent changes in applications, funding, and amounts received.

These trends help identify changes in loan demand over the reporting period.

### 4. Loan Applications by Purpose

The dashboard compares applications across purposes such as:

- Debt consolidation
- Credit card
- Home improvement
- Major purchase
- Small business
- Medical
- Car
- Education
- Wedding
- Vacation

Debt consolidation has the highest application count in the displayed purpose chart, with **18,214 applications**. Credit card loans follow with **4,998 applications**.

This analysis helps identify the most common reasons borrowers apply for loans.

### 5. Loan Term Analysis

The dashboard compares applications across two loan terms:

| Loan Term | Applications |
|---|---:|
| 36 months | 28,237 |
| 60 months | 10,339 |

The 36-month term represents the larger share of applications in the displayed chart.

Comparing loan terms can help the business understand borrower preferences and assess the portfolio's repayment structure.

### 6. Employment Length Analysis

The dashboard segments loan applications by employment length.

- Employees with **10+ years** of employment have the highest displayed application count, at **8,870**.
- Applicants with less than one year of employment account for **4,575 applications**.
- Applicants with three years of employment account for **4,088 applications**.

This breakdown helps explore how loan applications are distributed across employment-tenure groups.

### 7. Loan Status Analysis

The summary dashboard compares loan applications, funded amounts, amounts received, interest rates, and DTI across loan statuses.

The displayed categories include:

- Fully Paid
- Current
- Charged Off

The dashboard reports that fully paid loans account for most of the applications and amounts received shown. Charged-off loans remain relevant for monitoring credit risk and potential losses.

### 8. Geographic Analysis

The overview dashboard uses a US map to display loan applications by state.

This allows users to compare application distribution across locations and identify states with higher application volumes.

Geographic application counts can help identify lending concentration, but they do not independently indicate profitability or credit risk.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Monitor month-over-month changes in applications, funded amounts, and amounts received.
- Review debt consolidation and credit card loans because they represent major application purposes in the displayed data.
- Track charged-off loans and evaluate their contribution to the overall loan portfolio.
- Compare interest rates and DTI ratios across loan grades, purposes, and statuses.
- Review geographic concentration to understand the distribution of lending activity.
- Compare loan terms and employment-length groups to identify useful patterns for further analysis.
